# Opportunity Gap Framework

Identify opportunities that join evidence, product truth, and executable testing.

## Required Intersection

Retain an opportunity only when it has:

1. **User evidence** — repeated questions, objections, desired outcomes, workarounds, search language, or high-intent behavior.
2. **Competitive opening** — an unmet question, weak proof, saturated expression, underserved persona, missing format, or poorly served user moment.
3. **Product fit** — a truthful capability, workflow, proof source, or positioning advantage.
4. **Testability** — a concrete content execution that can be produced and evaluated.

If one element is missing, label the idea `needs validation` rather than an opportunity.

## Gap Types

- **Message gap** — competitors have the capability but explain it poorly.
- **Proof gap** — claims exist, but credible demonstrations or outcomes are weak.
- **Persona gap** — a relevant user group is rarely addressed.
- **Moment gap** — content ignores a high-pressure, high-frequency, or emotionally salient situation.
- **Format gap** — useful information exists but is not expressed in a platform-native format.
- **Objection gap** — users repeatedly ask a question that content does not answer.
- **Workflow gap** — competitors show a feature but not the end-to-end job.
- **Trust gap** — privacy, accuracy, pricing, reliability, or compatibility concerns lack evidence.
- **Distribution gap** — a relevant community or platform is underused; mark this cautiously when access data is incomplete.

## Scoring

Score each dimension from 1 to 5:

| Dimension | Weight | High score means |
|---|---:|---|
| User evidence | 30% | Multiple independent, high-intent signals exist |
| Product relevance and proof | 25% | The product can deliver and demonstrate the promise truthfully |
| Competitive differentiation | 20% | The opening is meaningful rather than cosmetic |
| Testability | 15% | A small, clear test can be run quickly |
| Repeatability | 10% | The idea can become a series or reusable system |

```text
opportunity_score = weighted average on a 100-point scale - risk penalty
```

Risk penalties:

- low: 0
- medium: 10
- high: 25

Do not hide the component scores behind the total.

## Opportunity Card

Record:

- Opportunity statement.
- Gap type.
- Target persona and user moment.
- Supporting user evidence.
- Supporting competitor/content evidence.
- Product truth and available proof.
- Proposed content expression.
- First controlled test.
- Success signal and observation window.
- Dependencies.
- Risks and prohibited claims.
- Component scores, total, evidence label, and confidence.

## Rejection Rules

Reject or defer an idea when:

- It depends on a capability the product does not have.
- It copies a competitor creative rather than transferring a principle.
- It has no traceable user evidence.
- It relies on one viral post without comparison.
- It cannot be distinguished from the category's existing content.
- It requires an unsupported performance, health, financial, legal, or safety claim.
- The team cannot produce or measure the proposed test.
